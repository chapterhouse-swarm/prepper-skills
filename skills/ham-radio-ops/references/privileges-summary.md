# U.S. amateur privileges (summary)

Checked 2026-11-18 against 47 CFR 97.301, 97.305, 97.313 (eCFR). Contiguous U.S. (ITU Region 2). Machine-readable version: `../data/us_amateur_privileges.json`.

CW (Morse) is allowed anywhere a class has privileges. Power limit is 1,500 W PEP unless noted, and always the minimum necessary.

## Technician (and Novice)

- **All amateur bands above 50 MHz**, all modes (CW-only at 50.0-50.1 and 144.0-144.1 MHz). Novice: only 222-225 MHz (25 W) and 1270-1295 MHz (5 W).
- HF, 200 W PEP:
  - 80 m 3.525-3.600, 40 m 7.025-7.125, 15 m 21.025-21.200: **CW only**
  - 10 m 28.0-28.3: CW, RTTY/data
  - 10 m 28.3-28.5: CW, **SSB voice**

## Advanced

As General, plus: 75 m phone from 3.700; 40 m from 7.025 continuous (phone 7.125-7.300); 20 m phone from 14.175; 15 m phone from 21.225.

## Amateur Extra

All amateur privileges: 80/75 m 3.500-4.000 (data 3.500-3.600, phone 3.600-4.000); 40 m 7.000-7.300 (phone from 7.125); 20 m 14.000-14.350 (phone from 14.150); 15 m 21.000-21.450 (phone from 21.200).

## 60 m (General, Advanced, Extra)

- Channels centered on 5.332, 5.348, 5.373, 5.405 MHz: 100 W ERP
- Segment 5.3515-5.3665 MHz: 9.15 W ERP
- Phone (upper sideband), CW, data. Secondary use; yield to government stations.

## Not modeled

Alaska, Hawaii and territories; stations near specific government sites (70 cm, 33 cm limits); bands above 10.5 GHz. Check 97.301-97.313 for those.
