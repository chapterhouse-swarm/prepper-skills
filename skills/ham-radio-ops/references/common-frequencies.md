# Common frequencies

Checked 2026-11-18. Amateur calling frequencies are widely used conventions from the ARRL band plan, not FCC rules. Always check `scripts/band_check.py` for your class before transmitting.

## Amateur (Technician and up)

| Band | Frequency (MHz) | Use |
|---|---|---|
| 2 m | **146.520** | National FM simplex calling frequency. The first place to try without a repeater. |
| 70 cm | 446.000 | National FM simplex calling |
| 1.25 m | 223.500 | FM simplex calling |
| 6 m | 52.525 | FM simplex calling |
| 10 m | 29.600 | FM simplex calling (General and up; Technicians can't use FM here) |

HF nets and emergency frequencies change by region and event. Get them from a region pack, your local ARES/RACES group, or by listening.

## License-free and weather

| Service | Frequency | Use |
|---|---|---|
| CB | Channel 9 (27.065 MHz) | Emergencies, traveler assistance |
| GMRS/FRS | Channel 20 (462.675 MHz) | Informal emergency convention among GMRS users (GMRS license to use repeaters/high power) |
| NOAA Weather Radio | 162.400, 162.425, 162.450, 162.475, 162.500, 162.525, 162.550 | Listen only: official warnings |
