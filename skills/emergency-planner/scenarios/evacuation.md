# Evacuation

Sources: Ready.gov Evacuation, Ready.gov Build a Kit.

## Do now
1. Follow the route officials give; shortcuts may be blocked or dangerous.
2. Grab the go-bag, medications, chargers, glasses, important documents, cash, and pet supplies.
3. If there is time and officials advise it: shut off utilities they tell you to, lock up, and leave a note on where you went.
4. Tell someone outside the area where you're going.

## Go-bag check (from inventory)
Compare the inventory against Ready.gov's basic kit list: water and non-perishable food for several days, flashlight, batteries, weather radio, first aid kit, whistle, dust masks, plastic sheeting and duct tape, moist towelettes and garbage bags, wrench or pliers for utilities, manual can opener, local maps, phone charger and backup battery. Add: medications, glasses, infant and pet supplies, documents, cash.

List what's missing, most important first.

## Get help when
- You have no transportation: contact local emergency management or 911 for assistance.
