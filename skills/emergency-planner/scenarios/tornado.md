# Tornado

Sources: NWS Tornado Safety, Ready.gov Tornadoes.

## Tornado WARNING: act immediately
1. Go to a basement or storm shelter. If none, go to a small interior room on the lowest floor, away from windows (closet, bathroom, hallway).
2. Get under something sturdy; cover your head and neck with arms, a mattress, blankets, or helmets.
3. Mobile homes and vehicles are not safe. Go to a sturdy building or shelter before the storm if you can.
4. Bring shoes, a flashlight, a phone, and the weather radio if they're within reach. Do not go back for things.

## Tornado WATCH: prepare
- Keep a weather radio and phone alerts on. Know where you'll shelter.
- Put shoes, a flashlight and the go-bag in the shelter spot.

## After
- Stay out of damaged buildings. Watch for downed power lines (35+ feet away), broken glass, nails, and gas smells.
- If you smell gas: leave, then report it from outside.
- Use text messages to check in; keep phone lines open for emergencies.
- Then switch to `power-outage.md` or `water-outage.md` as needed.

## Get help when
- Anyone is trapped or injured: call 911. Don't move seriously injured people unless they are in immediate danger.
