# Extended power outage

Sources: Ready.gov Power Outages, USDA FSIS, CDC/CPSC generator safety. Rules: `../references/safety-rules.md`, `../references/food-safety.md`.

## Do now (first hour)
1. Check on people who depend on powered medical devices or refrigerated medication; start their backup plan first.
2. Keep refrigerator and freezer closed. Note the time the power went out.
3. Switch to flashlights; check batteries and the weather radio.
4. Unplug sensitive electronics to avoid damage when power returns; leave one lamp on to know when it is back.
5. Report the outage to the utility if phones work.

## Next 24 hours
- At 4 hours: move perishables to a cooler with ice, or follow the discard rules.
- Generator: outdoors only, 20+ feet from doors/windows/vents, never backfed into a wall outlet.
- Heat or cold: see `winter-storm.md`, or in hot weather use cooler rooms, fluids, and a cooling center if officials open one.
- Conserve phone battery; charge from a running vehicle only when it is parked outdoors, never in a garage, even with the door open.

## Inventory checks
light, power (batteries, power banks, fuel), water, food that needs no cooking, cooler/ice, CO alarm, weather radio, medical.

## Get help when
- Someone's medical device or medication can't be kept running or cold.
- CO alarm sounds or several people feel sick: everyone outside, call 911.
- Indoor temperature becomes unsafe and you can't fix it: go to a warming/cooling center or another home.
