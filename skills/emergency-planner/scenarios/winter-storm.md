# Winter storm, ice storm, or heating loss

Sources: Ready.gov Winter Weather, CDC/CPSC carbon monoxide guidance. Rules: `../references/safety-rules.md`.

## Do now (first hour)
1. Stay indoors. Avoid travel unless officials say it is safe.
2. Pick one interior room to keep warm; close off unused rooms; cover windows; put towels at door gaps.
3. Dress everyone in layers, hats and dry socks; use blankets and sleeping bags.
4. Confirm the CO alarm works before using any fuel-burning heat source.
5. Fill containers with water in case pipes freeze.

## Next 24 hours
- Never use a grill, camp stove, generator or oven to heat the home.
- Space heaters: clearance from anything that burns, plug into the wall, off when sleeping.
- To help prevent frozen pipes, let faucets drip and open cabinet doors under sinks.
- Eat regular meals and drink fluids; avoid alcohol.
- Watch for signs of hypothermia and frostbite, especially in children and older adults (shivering, confusion, slurred speech, numb or pale skin).

## Inventory checks
heat (blankets, layers, safe heat source), CO alarm, water, food that needs no cooking, light, power, salt/sand, shovel.

## Get help when
- Anyone shows confusion, slurred speech, or will not stop shivering: call 911 if reachable.
- The warm room cannot be kept safely warm: go to a warming center or another home when travel is safe.
