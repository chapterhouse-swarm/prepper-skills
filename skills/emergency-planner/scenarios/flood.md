# Flood

Sources: NWS Turn Around Don't Drown, Ready.gov Floods.

## Do now
1. If told to evacuate, go. Use `evacuation.md`.
2. If water is rising and you can't leave, move to the highest level of the building. Do not go into a closed attic where you could be trapped; go to the roof only if necessary and signal for help.
3. **Turn Around, Don't Drown.** Never walk, swim or drive through floodwater. Moving water and hidden damage are deadly even when shallow.
4. Stay away from electrical equipment if you're wet or standing in water.

## After
- Return only when officials say it is safe.
- Treat floodwater as contaminated (sewage, chemicals). Wear boots and gloves; wash hands with clean water.
- Throw out food that touched floodwater unless it is in a sealed, undamaged commercial can (see `../references/food-safety.md`).
- Watch for downed lines, gas leaks, and structural damage. Photograph damage for insurance.

## Inventory checks
go-bag, water, food, light, weather radio, boots, gloves, cleaning supplies, important documents in a waterproof bag.

## Get help when
- Anyone is trapped by water: call 911 and get to the highest point.
