# Boil-water notice or water outage

Sources: CDC, EPA, Ready.gov Water. Numbers: `../references/water.md`; script: `scripts/supply_calc.py --bleach`.

## Do now (first hour)
1. Read the notice exactly: "boil water" (water is usable after treatment) vs "do not drink" or "do not use" (do not treat; use other water).
2. Stop using tap water for drinking, cooking, brushing teeth, baby formula, and ice until the notice says otherwise.
3. Count stored water against 1 gallon per person per day.
4. If water is still running and the notice allows, fill clean containers.

## Next 24 hours
- Treat water by boiling (rolling boil 1 minute; 3 minutes above 6,500 ft) or with bleach per the table. Never guess a bleach dose.
- Wash hands with bottled/treated water or use hand sanitizer.
- Throw away ice made from tap water during the notice.
- Sanitation if toilets can't flush: follow the local utility's guidance; keep waste sealed and away from water and food.

## Inventory checks
water, bleach (unscented, check date), fuel for boiling, clean containers, sanitizer, sanitation supplies.

## Get help when
- The notice says "do not use" and you run low on stored water: contact officials for water distribution points.
- Anyone has severe vomiting or diarrhea, especially infants or older adults.
